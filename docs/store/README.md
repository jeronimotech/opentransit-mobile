# Store listing screenshots

Screenshots for the App Store and Google Play, captured from the real app
running against **production** (`https://api.opentransit.tech`, Bogotá) so the
maps, the routes, the arrival times and the fares are all real. Nothing here is
a mockup, a fixture or a device frame. The listing copy that goes with them is
in `../STORE-LISTING.md`.

Captured on **2026-09-07, ~23:00 Bogotá time**, when the live feed was carrying
2,100–2,700 vehicles rather than the ~3,500 of a weekday peak. The numbers on
screen ("21 vehículos", "11 buses en 600 m", departures after 23:00) are that
hour's truth. A daytime re-run will show busier maps and shorter waits.

Everything under `ios/`, `ipad/` and `android/` is a 24-bit PNG with **no alpha
channel** — both stores reject alpha, and `simctl io … screenshot` writes RGBA,
so the iOS folders are flattened after capture (see *Regenerating*).

## The six screens

They are ordered as a story, and they follow the standing rule in
`../../../UX-AUDIT.md` — *the map is the product*: four of the six are
map-first, and the two list screens are the ones where the data itself is the
point.

| # | File | What it shows |
|---|------|---------------|
| 1 | `01_home_map.png` | Map-first home at street zoom: live fleet on the map, the search pill, the sheet only peeking |
| 2 | `02_search_address.png` | Planning from an address — the live geocoder answering a half-typed Bogotá address |
| 3 | `03_results.png` | Itineraries grouped by scenario (fastest / least walking / fewest transfers) with leave-by countdowns and the fare |
| 4 | `04_itinerary_live.png` | The chosen itinerary: route drawn on the map, fare breakdown, leg-by-leg timeline |
| 5 | `05_near_me.png` | "Cerca de mí" / "Near me": every live bus within 600 m, filtered by component |
| 6 | `06_locate_bus.png` | "Ubica tu bus" / "Locate your bus": pick a route at a stop and watch the buses coming to it |

**The iPad set ships only 1, 4, 5 and 6.** The app has no tablet layout yet: the
two list screens (2 and 3) render a phone-width column of rows on a 13" canvas
and leave the bottom two thirds blank. Those captures are kept under
`ipad/_excluded/` as evidence of the gap rather than shipped — a screenshot that
advertises empty space is worse than one screenshot fewer, and Apple's minimum
is one per localization. Restore them here once the tablet layout lands.

## What each store wants

Checked against the vendors' own documentation on **2026-09-07** — both change
these, so re-check before an upload rather than trusting this table.

### Apple — App Store Connect

Source: [Screenshot specifications](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/screenshot-specifications/)

* **1 to 10 screenshots** per localization, `.png`/`.jpg`/`.jpeg`, **no alpha
  channel**.
* **iPhone 6.9" is required** if the app runs on iPhone. Accepted portrait
  sizes: **1320 × 2868**, **1290 × 2796** or **1260 × 2736**. We ship
  **1320 × 2868** (iPhone 17 Pro Max).
* 6.5" (1284 × 2778 / 1242 × 2688) is *"Required if app runs on iPhone and
  screenshots for 6.9" display aren't provided"* — providing 6.9" makes it
  unnecessary.
* 6.3", 6.1", 5.5" and smaller are optional; Apple scales them down from the
  sizes above, so we deliberately do not generate them.
* **iPad 13" is required** if the app runs on iPad — and this app does
  (`TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY = "1,2"`). Portrait: **2064 × 2752**.

### Google — Play Console

Source: [Preview assets, screenshots and videos](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151)

* **Minimum two screenshots across different device types**, up to 8 per device
  type. **JPEG or 24-bit PNG (no alpha).**
* Minimum dimension 320 px, maximum 3840 px, and — the rule that bites —
  *"The maximum dimension of your screenshot can't be more than twice as long
  as the minimum dimension."*
* For promotional eligibility Play wants *"at least four screenshots with
  minimum 1080px resolution"* at **9:16 portrait (minimum 1080 × 1920)**.

A raw capture from any current phone fails that 2× rule — a Pixel 9 Pro is
1280 × 2856 (2.23×) and an iPhone 17 Pro Max 1320 × 2868 (2.17×). So the
Android captures are scaled to fit **1080 × 1920** and the leftover strips are
filled with the screenshot's own top-edge colour by `tool/store_resize.py`.
That satisfies both the 2× rule and the 9:16 promo requirement in one step.

Also on the Play checklist, not produced here: app icon 512 × 512 32-bit PNG
(with alpha) and feature graphic 1024 × 500 JPEG/24-bit PNG (no alpha).

## Regenerating

Everything is driven by `integration_test/store_shots_test.dart`, which walks
the six screens and prints a `SCREENSHOT:<name>` cue at each one;
`tool/screenshots.sh` greps for those cues and grabs the frame. The walkthrough
navigates by widget key and by router URL, never by a translated string, so the
same file produces both languages.

### iOS

```sh
tool/store_shots.sh "iPhone 17 Pro Max" es docs/store/ios/es
tool/store_shots.sh "iPhone 17 Pro Max" en docs/store/ios/en
tool/store_shots.sh "iPad Pro 13-inch (M5)" es docs/store/ipad/es
tool/store_shots.sh "iPad Pro 13-inch (M5)" en docs/store/ipad/en

# simctl writes RGBA; App Store Connect refuses an alpha channel.
for d in docs/store/ios/es docs/store/ios/en docs/store/ipad/es docs/store/ipad/en; do
  tool/store_resize.py "$d" "$d" keep
done
```

Any simulator whose native size is one Apple accepts for 6.9" works —
iPhone 17 Pro Max and 16 Pro Max give 1320 × 2868, iPhone 15 Pro Max
1290 × 2796, iPhone Air 1260 × 2736.

`tool/store_shots.sh` boots the named simulator, writes its system language
(`es-CO` / `en-US`) into the device's `.GlobalPreferences.plist` while it is
shut down, pins the status bar to 9:41 with full signal and a charged battery,
runs the walkthrough against production, then shuts the simulator back down if
it was not already running. It fails loudly if the named device does not exist,
rather than falling back to a different screen size.

### Android

```sh
tool/store_shots_android.sh Pixel_9_Pro es docs/store/android-raw/es
tool/store_shots_android.sh Pixel_9_Pro en docs/store/android-raw/en
tool/store_resize.py docs/store/android-raw/es docs/store/android/es 1080x1920
tool/store_resize.py docs/store/android-raw/en docs/store/android/en 1080x1920
```

The emulator script is the same walkthrough with `adb exec-out screencap` in
place of `simctl io`, plus SystemUI demo mode for the clean status bar and
`adb emu geo fix` for the simulated position. SystemUI drops demo mode whenever
something it owns changes — the app asking for location is enough — so the
script re-applies it immediately before every capture, not once at boot. Unlike
iOS there is no reliable way to switch an emulator's *system* language from a
script, so the Android status bar stays in the emulator's language; only the app
follows `SHOT_LOCALE`. Nothing in the status bar is words, so this is invisible.

### Knobs

* `API_URL=… tool/store_shots.sh …` — point at a different backend (defaults to
  `https://api.opentransit.tech`).
* `OUT=… tool/screenshots.sh <udid> <target>` — the generic capture script now
  honours an output directory, which is how the store folders are kept apart
  from `docs/screenshots/`.

## House rules for these images

* **Real data only.** The walkthrough talks to production; if Bogotá is quiet
  the shots show a quiet Bogotá. Do not re-run until the numbers flatter us.
* **No personal data.** Origins and destinations are public landmarks
  (Av. Carrera 7 # 32-16, Portal Norte). No saved home/work place, no favourite,
  no recent trip belonging to a real person may appear.
* **No admin panel, no API key, no token** in any frame.
* Captures are device-native and unretouched — no marketing captions, no device
  frames, no upscaling. Apple wants native pixels; the only transform applied
  anywhere is the Play 9:16 fit described above.
